Local Voice Reader Milestone 3 — UX Polish

The Unique Kernel

Transforming the side panel from a passive audio readout into an interactive, synchronized reading companion: clickable sentence-level scrubbing that updates both side panel and webpage highlights in real time, tab-origin awareness with instant tab switching, a collapsible mini-player mode, volume control, and zero-latency replay powered by in-memory audio chunk caching.

Six Key Enhancements

🌓

Light & Dark Theme

Unified sun/moon toggle at top-right of popup and side panel. Persisted in storage and strictly using semantic color tokens.

🔊

Volume Control Popup

Dedicated volume icon button at bottom right that reveals a smooth slider popover, matching the Speed control pattern.

🏷️

Tab Origin & Focus

Header displays which browser tab audio is reading from. Clicking the tab name immediately brings that tab back into focus.

🗕

Collapsible Mini-Player

One-click mini-player toggle that tucks away the full text reader into a compact strip while speech continues without pause.

🔁

Instant Replay & Caching

Play button becomes Replay at end of speech. In-memory chunk cache allows instant re-listening without re-generation lag.

👆

Clickable Sentences

Side panel highlights sentences in sync with webpage. Clicking any sentence immediately seeks playback and moves page focus.

Project Boundary

What "Working" Looks Like

A quick 1-to-2-minute screen recording demonstrating:

  • Toggling between Light and Dark mode in popup and side panel, persisting after reload.
  • Starting reading, switching tabs, and clicking the header tab pill to jump back to the reading tab.
  • Reading aloud with sentences highlighting in lockstep on both the webpage and the side panel.
  • Clicking an earlier or later sentence to immediately seek audio and update the page highlight.
  • Adjusting volume via the bottom-right popup slider.
  • Collapsing the panel into a mini-player strip without interrupting audio, then expanding it back.
  • Reaching the end of the text, seeing the Replay button, and clicking it to replay instantly from cache.

Included in Scope

  • Theme Persistence: Light/Dark toggle in popup and side panel, stored in browser.storage.local, using semantic CSS tokens.
  • Originating Tab Indicator: Side panel header shows origin tab title; clicking focuses that tab.
  • Clickable Sentence Reader: Synced sentence highlighting in panel with click-to-seek jump.
  • Collapsible Mini-Player: Side panel toggle shrinking reader to compact bar while audio plays.
  • Volume Control Popup: Bottom-right icon opening a volume slider popup.
  • Replay & Caching: Replay button state; in-memory audio chunk caching (10-min TTL / clear on new reading / panel close).

Future Additions

  • Global hardware media keys (Play/Pause, Next/Previous sentence).
  • Offscreen document audio worker to allow playback even if native side panel is closed completely.
  • Visual audio waveform scrubber.

Explicitly Cut

  • Persistent on-disk audio caching (IndexedDB/OPFS): Cut to avoid disk bloat and ensure zero residual trace for privacy.
  • Simultaneous multi-tab audio mixing: Cut to keep audio playback deterministic and avoid overlapping audio streams.
