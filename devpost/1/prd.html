Features and Acceptance Criteria

  • With text selected, the right-click menu shows Read Aloud.
  • Choosing it opens the side panel and starts the reading flow.
  • Choosing it on different text mid-reading replaces the old reading.
  • The sentence being heard is the one highlighted on the page.
  • When a sentence ends, the highlight moves on with no noticeable audio gap.
  • When the reading ends, the highlight is cleared.
  • Buffering state while audio is prepared.
  • Pause icon while playing; pressing it pauses.
  • Play icon while paused; pressing it resumes where it paused.
  • Clicking Speed opens a slider popup above the button.
  • 0.25x is audibly slower; 2x is audibly faster.
  • Clicking Voice opens a list popup above the button.
  • The single available voice is shown and selected.
  • Light (white and navy) and dark themes switch via named color roles, no hardcoded colors.

States and Boundaries

Top of the panel shows "Downloading the voice ....". Playback starts after the download.
No download message. Only the brief buffering state and the "Playing" toast.
"You seem to be offline, check connection"
"Encounter some error, while fetching voice"
The voice stops after the last sentence and the highlight clears. A new selection replaces the current reading.

The Boundary

BuildingRight-click Read Aloud, music-player side panel, local voice with first-run download, sentence reading with look-ahead, on-page highlight, Play/Pause, Speed, Voice popup, light/dark themes, status and error messages.

DeferredSkip / rewind / forward, separate Stop button, more voices, reader mode.

Non-goalsCloud text-to-speech, accounts or sync, bundling the voice inside the extension.
